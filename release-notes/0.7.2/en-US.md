# ScrapeFun 0.7.2

Jellyfin compatibility and virtual libraries are now available on the free plan.

## Improvements

- Existing virtual libraries, media, and viewing progress are preserved. Administrators can enable Jellyfin compatibility on free servers.
- Sign in to your server on the local network with a local account. Among the new plans, Pro Family includes invitations and remote sharing for up to five people, including the owner.
- Existing annual and quarterly benefits and renewals continue.

## Upgrading

The update restarts your server. Back up your data and settings first. Older servers continue to apply their existing rules for Jellyfin and virtual libraries.
