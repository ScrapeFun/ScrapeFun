# ScrapeFun 0.6.2

This update adds a personal theme accent color and improves account-based connections.

## Improvements

- Choose a theme accent color in Personal Settings. Your saved color is available when you sign in to the same server with the same user account on another device.
- Improved session handling when a client connects to the server through a ScrapeFun account.

## Before updating

- The update restarts the server. Back up your data and settings, and choose a time when playback and background jobs are idle.
