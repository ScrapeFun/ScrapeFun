# ScrapeFun 0.6.1

This update improves setup, metadata loading, reading, and playback.

## Improvements

- The server tries a fallback address when a direct connection to the TMDB API or image service fails.
- First-time setup can recover completed account authorization and gives clearer feedback on storage addresses and library settings.
- Improved recovery when opening books, scrolling comics, and switching video episodes.

## Before updating

- The update restarts the server. Back up your data and settings, and choose a time when playback and background jobs are idle.
