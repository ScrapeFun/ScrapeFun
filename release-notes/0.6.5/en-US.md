# ScrapeFun 0.6.5

This Docker update reduces the initial image size and improves library navigation and account connections.

## Improvements

- Subtitle fonts and components for speech recognition and inference download on first start.
- Reconnecting after sign-out and returning to the home screen now produces less flicker.
- Continue Watching cards load more smoothly, and switching to media details is smoother.

## Before updating

- The update restarts the server. Back up your data and configuration first.
- The container needs internet access for the initial component downloads. Wait for setup to finish before signing in.
